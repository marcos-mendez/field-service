Checklist sheets for the equipments of a preventive visit.

The activities of the template of each checklist line are copied to the
line, where the worker answers them one by one: approved, reproved or
not applicable for checks, yes or no for questions. A line can only be
marked as done once every activity is answered; the worker who validates
it and the conclusion of the sheet are recorded, with an optional
signature.

Each preventive visit type can open a corrective order for the equipment
when its sheet is reproved, listing only the reproved activities, and
notify a responsible user.

Usage
=====

On a checklist line of a service order, open the sheet and answer each
activity of its template: *Approved*, *Reproved* or *Not Applicable* for
checks, *Yes* or *No* for questions. The line can be validated once
every activity is answered; the user who validated it is recorded, with
an optional signature.

On a preventive visit type, *Corrective Order When Reproved* opens one
corrective order per reproved equipment, of the given order type,
listing the reproved activities, and schedules an activity for the
responsible user.
