Checklist sheets for the equipments of a preventive visit.

The activities of the template of each checklist line are copied to the line,
where the worker answers them one by one: approved, reproved or not applicable
for checks, yes or no for questions. A line can only be marked as done once
every activity is answered; the worker who validates it and the conclusion of
the sheet are recorded, with an optional signature.

Each preventive visit type can open a corrective order for the equipment when
its sheet is reproved, listing only the reproved activities, and notify a
responsible user.
