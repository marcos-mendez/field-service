On a checklist line of a service order, open the sheet and answer each activity
of its template: *Approved*, *Reproved* or *Not Applicable* for checks, *Yes*
or *No* for questions. The line can be validated once every activity is
answered; the user who validated it is recorded, with an optional signature.

On a preventive visit type, *Corrective Order When Reproved* opens one
corrective order per reproved equipment, of the given order type, listing the
reproved activities, and schedules an activity for the responsible user.
